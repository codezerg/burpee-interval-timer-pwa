BURPEE INTERVAL TIMER - PROPOSAL
Personal installable web app (PWA)
Date: 2026-10-21
================================================================

1. GOAL
----------------------------------------------------------------
A simple phone app for doing burpees at a steady pace and getting
better over time. Inspired by the Busy Dad Program, but flexible:
short custom workouts instead of fixed 20-minute sessions.

Core idea: a workout = number of burpees + seconds per burpee.
The app beeps at the start of each burpee so you keep the pace.
  - Beginner:       1 burpee every 15 s
  - Very fit:       1 burpee every 4-5 s
Progress = slowly lowering the seconds and/or raising the count.


2. FEATURES
----------------------------------------------------------------
2.1 Custom workouts
  - Create, edit, delete workouts
  - Fields: burpee type, number of burpees,
    seconds per burpee (step 0.5 s, range 2-60 s)
  - No name to type: the title is built automatically as
    "{count} {type} burpees", e.g. "40 Standard burpees",
    "5 Navy SEAL burpees". Pace is shown under the title.
  - Burpee types:
      Standard       (squat, plank, push-up, jump)
      6-count        (military: no jump; conditioning)
      Navy SEAL      (3 push-ups + 2 knee drives; strength)
      Other          (your own name, e.g. "No push-up")
    Paces are very different between types, so progress
    and records are tracked separately per type.
  - How-to description: when choosing a type, the app shows
    step-by-step instructions, a suggested starting pace and
    common mistakes. Also reachable from an "i" (info) button
    on the workout and run screens. Text for "Other" types is
    written by you.

    STANDARD BURPEE
      1. Stand, feet shoulder-width apart
      2. Squat down, hands on the floor
      3. Kick feet back into a plank
      4. Do one push-up (chest to floor)
      5. Jump feet back to your hands
      6. Jump up, hands overhead
      Beginner pace: 12-15 s.  Fit: 4-5 s.
      Easier: step back/in instead of kicking, skip the jump.
      Mistakes: sagging hips in plank, landing stiff-legged.

    6-COUNT (MILITARY) BURPEE
      1. Squat down, hands on the floor
      2. Kick feet back into a plank
      3. Lower chest to the floor (push-up down)
      4. Push up (push-up up)
      5. Jump feet back to your hands
      6. Stand up (no jump)
      Focus: conditioning. Beginner pace: 10-15 s.
      Mistakes: half push-ups, not standing fully upright.

    NAVY SEAL BURPEE
      1. Squat down, hands on the floor
      2. Kick feet back into a plank
      3. Push-up
      4. Left knee to left elbow, return
      5. Push-up
      6. Right knee to right elbow, return
      7. Push-up
      8. Feet back to hands, stand up
      Focus: strength (chest, shoulders, arms, core).
      Try it when you can do ~10 clean push-ups in a row.
      Beginner pace: 25-30 s.
      Easier: push-ups on your knees.
      Mistakes: hips sagging or piking during push-ups,
      rushing the knee drives.

    (Simple drawings/illustrations could be added later.)
  - Total time is calculated automatically
    e.g. 20 burpees x 15 s = 5:00
  - Example workouts included to start with:
      "40 Standard burpees"   every 15 s  (10:00)
      "20 6-count burpees"    every 12 s  (4:00)
      "5 Navy SEAL burpees"   every 30 s  (2:30)

2.2 Workout timer
  - "Get ready" countdown before start (default 5 s,
    short ticks on the last 3 seconds)
  - Beep at the start of every burpee
    (beep #1 at 0:00, beep #2 at 0:15, ... for a 15 s pace)
  - Distinct "finished" beep at the end
  - Big on-screen display:
      top row: elapsed | current burpee (e.g. 7 / 20) | pace
      BIG ring  = whole workout, total time left in the middle
      SMALL ring = current burpee, seconds to next beep
  - Pause / Resume / Stop. Paused screen keeps both rings,
    greyed and frozen, with a "PAUSED 0:42" pill; Resume
    continues with the same seconds left on the small ring.
  - Screen stays awake during the workout
  - Sound only, no vibration: a loud, clear beep is the
    signal for the next burpee

2.3 After a workout
  - Summary: burpees done, pace, duration
  - Burpee count can be corrected before saving
    (e.g. you missed a couple)
  - "How did it feel?" + "make it harder next time"
    suggestions (see 2.6 c), record banner (2.6 b)
  - Save or Discard

2.3a Too tired? Pause, stop, catch up
  - PAUSE at any time: timer and beeps freeze, resume when
    ready. Pause time is not counted as workout time.
  - STOP at any time: what you did is always recorded,
    e.g. "28 / 40 burpees" (marked as partial)
  - Catch-up: if a workout was stopped early, Home shows
      "12 burpees left from 40 Burpees today   [Catch up]"
    Tapping it starts a short workout with just the missing
    burpees at the same pace. You can do it later that day
    (or another day), and it is linked to the original
    session, so history shows e.g. "40 / 40 (28 + 12)".
  - Catch-up can be dismissed if you don't want to finish it
  - Everything done (partial or catch-up) counts toward the
    weekly goal

2.4 Weekly goal
  - Goal type is a setting: BURPEES, MINUTES or BOTH
  - Set target(s) per week (e.g. 150 burpees and/or 40 min)
  - Both: the week counts as hit when both targets are
    reached; Home shows burpees big and minutes underneath
  - All burpee types count the same (1 Navy SEAL = 1 burpee)
  - Week starts Monday or Sunday (setting)
  - Home screen shows progress bar + days you trained this week

2.5 History & progress
  - List of all sessions (date, workout, burpees, pace, time)
    with delete option
  - Last 8 weeks bar chart vs. weekly goal. Each bar is
    stacked by burpee type, one color per type, easiest at
    the bottom, going up in difficulty:
      bottom [ 6-count | Standard | Navy SEAL | Other ] top
    with a legend showing the total for each type
  - Records per burpee type: total burpees, best session,
    fastest pace; plus weeks the goal was reached
  - Filter history by burpee type

2.6 Motivation (ideas from WHOOP, Apple Fitness, Strava, Duolingo,
    Peloton, Garmin, Fitbod, Couch to 5K, Busy Dad Training)
  Rule: compare only with your past self, never guilt.
  Rest days are neutral grey, never red. No daily streaks.

  First version:
  a) Weekly streak: counts weeks where the weekly goal was hit
     ("4 wk" on Home). Earn 1 shield every 4 good weeks; a
     shield automatically covers one missed week.
     Recovery weeks count if the easier goal is hit; breaks
     (sick / holiday) freeze the streak (see 2.8).
  b) Record banner on the Complete screen, e.g.
     "New record: 40 Standard burpees without a pause (was 32)".
     Records per type: most in a session, fastest pace,
     longest without pause, best week.
  c) "How did it feel?"  Easy / OK / Hard  after every workout.
     2x Easy in a row -> suggest +1 burpee OR -0.5 s pace.
     Hard -> suggest repeating ("repeating is progress too").
     Max ~10% increase per week, to avoid overdoing it.
     (Replaces the plain "make it harder" buttons.)
  d) Milestone badges in History: 100 / 500 / 1k / 5k / 10k
     burpees, 10 / 25 / 50 / 100 workouts, per-type badges,
     streak badges. Next badge shown greyed with "948 / 1000".
     Badges can never be lost.
  e) Pace progress in History: "15.0 s -> 14.0 s, 7% faster
     since 1 Sep" + small line chart + trend arrows
     (last 4 weeks vs before).

  Later version:
  f) Monday review: shown on first open of a new week.
     Last week vs 4-week average, goal hit?, streak, highlight,
     status word: Building / Holding / Resting.
     Then "New week. Plan your sessions?": pick days + a cue
     ("After morning coffee"), shown on Home as today's plan.
  g) Levels per burpee type (new "Levels" tab), e.g. Standard:
       1 Starter  20 every 15 s     4 Strong    60 every 10 s
       2 Base     40 every 15 s     5 Fit       80 every  8 s
       3 Steady   40 every 12 s     Graduate   100 every  6 s
     Pass a test (no pausing) to level up. Take it when ready,
     retry anytime.
  h) Monthly challenge (pick your own, e.g. "hit weekly goal
     3 times", "Navy SEAL month: 200 SEALs") and a
     "Year in Burpees" recap in December.

  Catch-up is shown as a bonus ("+12 recovered"), not a debt,
  and is capped so it can't turn into one huge session.

2.7 Settings
  - Weekly goal type (Burpees / Minutes / Both) + targets,
    week start day
  - Countdown length, beep volume (+ test beep)
  - Recovery: Start a recovery week, "tell me it's time
    after" N weeks (default 4, or off), easy-week goal %
    (default 55%), ask about pain after Hard on/off,
    Take a break
  - Export data to a file (backup) / Import from file
  - Reset all data

2.8 Recovery & rest (based on the rest-week research)
  Why: burpees are hard on wrists, shoulders, knees, Achilles
  and lower back. Tendons and joints adapt slower than heart
  and muscles, so easier weeks matter for bodyweight training
  too, not only for weightlifting.

  Rule: the app tells you when it's time, but never starts a
  recovery week on its own. Until you tap Start, nothing
  changes.

  a) Recovery week reminder: after 4 training weeks since
     the last recovery week (setting: 4-6 weeks, or off),
     Home shows a teal card on Monday:
       "Time for a recovery week
        4 hard weeks in a row. An easy week (goal 80) lets
        your joints catch up. Streak stays safe."
       [Start recovery week]  [Not this week]
     "Not this week" hides it for the week; it comes back
     next Monday.
     You can take a recovery week ANY time, not only when
     it's due: the weekly goal card on Home always shows a
     small row, e.g.
       "Training week 3 of 4 · recovery week due soon
        [Start now]"
     (also Settings > Start a recovery week). Starting one
     early resets the 4-week count.
     Once started, it lasts until the end of the week; can
     be ended early.
     Goal drops to ~55% (150 -> 80). Same pace, about
     half the burpees. Home shows a teal "Recovery week" card
     with "easy versions" of your workouts (e.g. 20 instead of
     40), suggests more 6-count and fewer Navy SEAL.
     No level tests or "+1 burpee" suggestions that week.
     Afterwards: back to the previous goal, not higher.
     Hitting the easy goal keeps the streak; History labels
     the week "Recovery week" (never "missed").
  b) (removed: no extra prompts after Hard ratings; the
     Monday reminder in a) is the only one)
  c) "Anything hurting?" check after a Hard rating:
       Nothing, just tired | Wrists | Shoulders | Lower back |
       Knees | Ankles / Achilles
     Gives a gentler option for a few days:
       wrists/shoulders -> no push-up burpees, skip Navy SEAL
       knees/Achilles   -> step back/in, no jump
       lower back       -> slower pace, step back/in
     "Still hurts after 2 days, or sharp pain / swelling?
     Rest and get it checked." (general tips, not medical
     advice)
  d) Take a break (sick / holiday / injury / other):
     1 week, 2 weeks or until I'm back. Streak is frozen (no
     shields used), no goal, no reminders, weeks grey.
  e) Welcome back after time off, gentle restart:
       up to 1 week off       -> ~90% of previous goal
       2-3 weeks off          -> ~75%
       4+ weeks or after fever -> 50-60%, rebuild over 2-4 wk
     Shown as a short ramp (e.g. 110 -> 130 -> 150), same pace,
     fewer burpees. Can be skipped.
  Warning signs listed in the app: joint pain (not muscle
  soreness), soreness lasting week to week, easy workouts
  suddenly feeling hard, poor sleep, dreading workouts.


3. SCREENS
----------------------------------------------------------------
  [Home]      weekly goal card, list of workouts, + New workout
  [Editor]    name, burpees (- / +), seconds (- / +), total time
  [Run]       full-screen timer, Pause / Stop
  [Done]      summary, edit count, harder-next-time, Save
  [History]   8-week chart, records, session list
  [Paused]    both rings frozen + greyed, Resume / Stop & save
  [Settings]  goal (burpees/minutes/both), sound, recovery,
              data backup
  [Levels]    level roadmap per type, monthly challenge
  [Review]    Monday weekly review + plan the week
  [Recovery]  Home "time for a recovery week" card, Home in
              recovery-week mode, "Anything hurting?"
              sheet, Take a break sheet, Welcome back screen
  Bottom tabs: Home | History | Levels | Settings

Mockups: docs/mockups/index.html (open in a browser)


4. HOW IT WORKS (TECHNICAL)
----------------------------------------------------------------
  - Plain HTML + CSS + JavaScript, no frameworks, no server code
  - Files:
      index.html           the app
      manifest.json        name, icon, full-screen mode
      sw.js                service worker, works offline
      icon-192.png, icon-512.png, apple-touch-icon.png
  - Beeps generated with the Web Audio API (no sound files)
  - Screen kept on with the Wake Lock API
  - Data stored on the phone only (browser local storage)
  - Install: open the site on the phone -> "Add to Home Screen"
    (iPhone: Safari > Share button; Android: Chrome > Install app)


5. HOSTING
----------------------------------------------------------------
To be decided later. The app is just static files, so it runs
anywhere. Installing on a phone needs HTTPS. Options:
  - Netlify Drop: drag the folder onto app.netlify.com/drop
    (easiest, about 1 minute)
  - Your own container: any static web server (e.g. nginx or
    Caddy) serving the folder, behind HTTPS (Caddy does
    certificates automatically; or a reverse proxy you
    already have)
  - GitHub Pages / Cloudflare Pages
Can be tested on the computer first at http://localhost.


6. LIMITATIONS
----------------------------------------------------------------
  - Data lives on one phone; no sync. Use Export for backups.
    Clearing Safari/Chrome site data deletes it.
  - iPhone: the silent switch (Ring/Silent) mutes web-app
    beeps - keep it on Ring during workouts. The app shows a
    reminder before the first workout and has a Test beep.
  - The timer needs the app open on screen (it keeps the
    screen awake). Switching apps mid-workout may delay beeps.

8. DESIGN INSPIRATION (from the commercial app screenshots)
----------------------------------------------------------------
Keep / borrow:
  - Clean light look: white background, one bright accent
    color (orange), rounded white cards, big bold numbers
  - Home: big summary number at top (this week), workout cards
    showing "40 Burpees | 10 min", round orange "+" button
  - Workout detail: Duration / Burpees / Pace in large digits,
    big orange START button at the bottom
  - Get-ready screen: huge countdown number (3, 2, 1) with
    "Tap to start immediately"
  - Run screen layout:
      top row:  ELAPSED  |  BURPEE 7/40  |  TIME LEFT
      center:   ring that drains until the next beep,
                with seconds to next beep in large digits
      bottom:   big round Pause button, mute button
  - History: "This week" vs "Overall" switch, bar chart
    (days of the week / weeks / years), totals card
    (duration, burpees, workouts), "Latest activity" list
  - Bottom tab bar with icons: Home | History | Settings

Leave out (not needed for a personal app):
  - Library, Community, "Go Pro", sharing, accounts
  - Exercise animations (only one exercise: burpees)
  - Calories: optional rough estimate only, if wanted
    (number would be a guess anyway)

Adds over the commercial app:
  - Pace per burpee (seconds) is the main setting, not
    "laps x 15 s"; easy +/- adjustment for progression
  - Weekly goal with progress bar (they show totals only)
  - Records and "harder next time" buttons
  - Burpees done is saved, not just duration


9. DECISIONS (formerly open questions)
----------------------------------------------------------------
  1. Goal type: a setting - Burpees, Minutes or Both.
  2. Theme: light, as in the mockups. Dark mode maybe later.
  3. Phone: no vibration at all; sound (beep) only. Works on
     iPhone and Android.
  4. App name: working name "Burpee Timer" (easy to change).
  5. Hosting: decided later - Netlify, or your own container.
  6. Catch-up: offered until end of day by default
     (setting: end of day / until done or dismissed).
  7. Old history: not imported, start fresh.
  8. 1 Navy SEAL = 1 burpee toward the goal (kept simple,
     no setting).

Design status: done. Next step is Phase 1.
