How to do a Standard burpee
  1. Stand, feet shoulder-width apart
  2. Squat down, hands on the floor
  3. Kick feet back into a plank
  4. One push-up, chest to the floor
  5. Jump feet back to your hands
  6. Jump up, hands overhead
Beginner 12–15 s Fit 4–5 s
Easier: step back and in instead of jumping, skip the jump at the top. Watch out: sagging hips in the plank, stiff-legged landings.
